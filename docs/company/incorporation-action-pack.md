## Recommended Route

Default recommendation for this week: use Clerky standalone incorporation or
Clerky Company Lifetime Package, but pause officer-title and founder-stock
issuance choices until immigration counsel clears them.

Why: your deadline needs a legal registered entity by July 27. Clerky is
startup-standard and modular. It can handle formation and later post-incorp
docs without forcing every step tonight. Stripe Atlas is clean and cheaper, but
its all-in-one workflow is less ideal if you may need to slow down officer or
stock steps for G-4 review. Direct Delaware filing is fastest/leanest if you
want only the entity, but it leaves more legal paperwork for you or counsel.

Conservative operating rule:

- Form the entity.
- Do not pay yourself.
- Do not take revenue for now.
- Do not open operational bank/payment flows until counsel has cleared the
  immigration/activity posture.
- Treat officer titles, director service, EIN responsible party, founder stock,
  founder IP assignment, and bank account control as lawyer questions where
  G-4 status might matter.

## 83(b) Sacred Deadline

Plain language:

If you receive restricted founder stock that vests over time, the IRS normally
taxes the stock as it vests. An 83(b) election tells the IRS: tax me now based
on today's low value, rather than later as the stock vests. For a startup, this
can prevent ugly tax results if the company becomes valuable.

Deadline math:

- Day 0 is the date the restricted stock is transferred/issued/purchased.
- Count 30 calendar days, not business days.
- The IRS filing must be made no later than 30 days after transfer.
- Do not wait for "almost 30 days." Treat Day 10 as the internal deadline.

If stock is issued on July 15, 2026:

- Day 0: July 15, 2026
- Day 10 internal deadline: July 25, 2026
- Day 30 legal deadline: August 14, 2026

If stock is issued on July 22, 2026:

- Day 0: July 22, 2026
- Day 10 internal deadline: August 1, 2026
- Day 30 legal deadline: August 21, 2026

Reminder system:

1. The moment stock is issued, create four calendar events:
   - `83(b) Day 0 - stock issued today`
   - `83(b) Day 3 - prepare and review election`
   - `83(b) Day 7 - mail/file/send election, do not slip`
   - `83(b) Day 25 - emergency proof check`
2. Save these files in one folder:
   - signed stock purchase agreement
   - signed 83(b) election
   - proof of mailing or service filing receipt
   - company copy
   - any service confirmation from Atlas/Clerky
3. If using Clerky/Atlas, still independently calendar the deadline.

Copy/paste calendar event:

```text
Title: 83(b) election deadline for Palari founder stock
Date: [30 calendar days after stock issuance]
Notes: Sacred deadline. Confirm signed 83(b) election was filed with IRS and proof saved. Do not rely only on memory or service emails.
```
